# Lesson 02: Classification of Matter

## Learning Objectives

- Classify matter based on composition.
- Distinguish pure substances from mixtures.
- Differentiate homogeneous and heterogeneous mixtures.

---

## Why This Topic Matters

Not all matter is the same. Scientists classify matter to better understand its properties and behavior.

---

## Definition

Matter is broadly classified into:

- Pure Substances
- Mixtures

---

## Building the Intuition

Imagine a kitchen.

Sugar, distilled water and copper wire each have a fixed composition. They are pure substances.

Tea, milk and air contain more than one substance. They are mixtures.

---

## Key Concepts

### Pure Substances
- Fixed composition
- Uniform properties
- Includes elements and compounds

### Mixtures
- Variable composition
- Components retain their properties
- Can often be separated by physical methods

### Types of Mixtures

**Homogeneous:** Uniform throughout.
Examples: Air, salt solution, vinegar.

**Heterogeneous:** Non-uniform.
Examples: Sand and water, oil and water, soil.

---

## Examples and Counter-examples

| Pure Substance | Mixture |
|---|---|
|Gold|Air|
|Copper|Milk|
|Water|Sea water|
|Sodium chloride|Tea|

---

## Common Misconceptions

- Air is not an element; it is a homogeneous mixture.
- Milk is not a pure substance.
- Clear does not always mean pure.
