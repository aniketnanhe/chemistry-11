# Lesson 11: Dimensional Analysis

> **Estimated Review Time:** 10–15 minutes

# Learning Objectives
- Use conversion factors correctly.
- Convert between units.
- Verify equations using dimensions.

# Why This Topic Matters

Units are powerful error-checking tools. If the units don't work, the answer is probably wrong.

# Building the Intuition

Treat units like algebra.

For example:

100 cm × (1 m / 100 cm) = 1 m

The centimetres cancel naturally.

# Conversion Factors

Examples:
- 1 m = 100 cm
- 1 kg = 1000 g
- 1 L = 1000 mL

# Dimensional Consistency

A valid equation must have the same dimensions on both sides.

# Examples

- Convert 250 cm to metres.
- Convert 2.5 kg to grams.
- Convert 500 mL to litres.

# Common Misconceptions

- Multiply or divide units without checking cancellation.
- Ignore units during calculations.

# Think About It

1. Why do units cancel in dimensional analysis?
2. Can an equation be numerically correct but dimensionally incorrect?

# Summary

Dimensional analysis simplifies conversions and helps detect mistakes.

# Retrieval Practice

1. Convert 3.2 km to metres.
2. Convert 750 g to kilograms.
3. Explain dimensional consistency.
